How it decides

  • Reads the position and pool at one block; reports current tick, in-range and distance to the nearer bound.
  • Proposes ticks on the pool's spacing, at your width or two daily sigmas of 24 h realised volatility.
  • Amounts keep the same liquidity (V3 formula); passes Marque's MCS-REB-1 test.
  • Its own position re-centres when price leaves the range, drifts, the width changes, or a UTC day passes.
