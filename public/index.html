When it re-centres

Checked every four hours. At most three times a UTC day.

  1. 01

    The price leaves the range

    The position has stopped earning. Close it and reopen around the current tick.

  2. 02

    The centre drifts

    Price has moved more than a third of the half-width from the middle, so one side is running thin.

  3. 03

    Volatility changes the right width

    Width is two daily sigmas of mainnet USDT/WBNB's 24 h realised volatility. A 20% change resizes the range.

  4. 04

    A day passes

    A standing daily re-centre, so the range never goes stale on a quiet day.
